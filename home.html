DIGITAL GOVERNANCE PLATFORM

Modernizing Packaged Commodity Compliance Verification

The Smart Legal Metrology Inspection & Compliance System (SLM-ICS) provides authorized Legal Metrology officers and inspectors with a robust digital governance workspace. The system digitizes on-field and laboratory inspections of pre-packaged commodities, ensuring stringent enforcement of the Legal Metrology Act, 2009 and the Legal Metrology (Packaged Commodities) Rules, 2011.

Inspectors can capture high-resolution commodity labels, extract textual declarations using advanced optical character recognition, structure mandatory metadata, evaluate statutory rule compliance, catalog photographic evidence, and generate digitally signed inspection reports with full audit traceability.

Standardized Checks
Eliminates manual human error and subjective interpretation across inspection zones.
Accelerated Turnaround
Reduces inspection assessment time from hours to under 30 seconds per packaged item.

Institutional Governance Architecture

  • Pre-Packaged Commodity Verification

    Automated verification of Net Quantity, MRP (incl. taxes), Manufacturer address, Month/Year of packing, Unit Sale Price (USP), and Country of Origin.

  • Statutory Rule Engine

    Configurable backend rules verifying font sizes, declared quantities against Second Schedule standard units, and dual-unit packaging compliance.

  • Evidentiary Record Dossier

STANDARDIZED WORKFLOW

How Digital Inspection Operates

A step-by-step, transparent, and auditable 6-stage inspection protocol ensuring systematic verification from physical label capture to official certificate generation.

1

Capture / Upload

Inspector captures or uploads commodity label photos using mobile camera or optical scanner.

2

Image Processing

Interactive image normalization, perspective correction, contrast enhancement, and noise reduction.

3

OCR Extraction

Optical character recognition engine identifies raw text lines with localized bounding boxes.

4

AI Structuring

Machine intelligence maps raw strings into structured statutory fields with confidence ratings.

5

Rule Evaluation

Automated rules engine checks declarations against LMPC 2011 clauses and schedules.

6

Verification & Report

Authorized officer reviews findings, confirms decisions, and generates an official report.

Human-in-the-Loop Safeguard: All automated AI and OCR extractions serve as decision support tools. Final statutory determinations, show cause notices, and enforcement actions remain strictly under the authority of authorized Legal Metrology officers.

STATUTORY VERIFICATION

Compliance Intelligence Framework

Automated verification parameters aligned with the Packaged Commodities Rules, 2011. Configurable backend rules validate every mandatory declaration.

Rule 6(1)(a) Verified

Product Identity

Generic name of the commodity declared prominently on the Principal Display Panel.

Rule 6(1)(b) Verified

Declared Net Quantity

Standard weight, measure, or numerical count verified in SI units (g, kg, mL, L).

Rule 6(1)(e) Non-Compliant

MRP & Tax Clause

Maximum Retail Price declaration must explicitly include "Inclusive of all taxes".

Rule 6(1)(d) Review Req.

Manufacturer Address

Complete physical name and address of manufacturer, packer, or importer.

Rule 6(1)(a) Non-Compliant

Country of Origin

Mandatory declaration of manufacturing origin on all imported commodities.

Rule 6(1)(f) Verified

Packing / Import Date

Month and Year in which the commodity is manufactured or pre-packed.

Rule 6(1)(g) Verified

Unit Sale Price (USP)

Price per g/kg/mL/L prominently declared alongside the overall retail price.

Rule 9 Verified

Font Size Compliance

Minimum numeral and letter heights determined by package area in Table-I.

INSPECTOR ONBOARDING

Standard Operating Procedure Guide

Step-by-step guidance for authorized officers conducting field inspections through the digital platform.

Authorized inspectors log in using government credentials, complete multi-factor verification, and select their assigned enforcement zone or field station.

Capture clear, well-lit photographs of the Principal Display Panel (PDP) and mandatory declaration panels. Ensure the entire label is framed without excessive glare or perspective skew.

The platform automatically extracts text segments and highlights bounding polygons. Inspectors review bounding boxes to verify complete textual capture across multilingual declarations.

Extracted values are structured into MRP, Net Quantity, Dates, Origin, and Manufacturer. The rule engine validates each declaration against the Packaged Commodities Rules 2011.

The inspector reviews any flagged non-compliances, attaches additional physical evidence if required, specifies statutory remarks, and records an official determination.

The system generates a cryptographically signed Inspection Certificate or formal Show Cause Notice with a unique verification QR code and archives it in the central registry.
